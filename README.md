# Next

**Focus on the work, not the to-do list. Always know what’s next.**

Next is an experimental project exploring an end-to-end, theoretical product process using AI. It imagines a tool that captures commitments from everyday work conversations and helps people decide what to focus on next.

## The problem

In communication-heavy roles, tasks and commitments show up across meetings, messages, and email. Keeping track of them takes effort, and deciding what to tackle next can be just as challenging as capturing the work in the first place. The goal is to help people spend less time managing a to-do list and miss fewer important commitments.

## What I explored

- Clarifying the pain points, desired outcome, and product goal
- Defining a broad target segment, with a representative use case
- Conducting five informal user interviews to explore needs and workflows
- Comparing product directions and shaping a focused initial concept
- Exploring how tasks might be prioritized, explained, and reviewed
- Iterating on the design system and a clickable prototype based on feedback

## Prototype

[View the live prototype](https://melanieps1.github.io/next-prototype/)

The prototype uses illustrative sample data. Integrations and task detection are simulated; it does not connect real accounts or validate a production prioritization model.

## About this project

This is a learning experiment, not a production application or a claim that the product concept has been validated. The research is exploratory, and the prototype is intended to make the product hypothesis tangible and invite further feedback.
